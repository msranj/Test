### Neurodiversity PRINCIPLES
----------------------------
THINKING ABOUT BRAINS

#### 1. Neurotypical
    Brain functioning that is considered "typical" in processing and learning (A common acronym for
neurotypical is NT)


#### 2. Neurodivergent
    Brain functioning that differs from what is considered "typical" in processing and learning (A common acronym for neurodivergent is ND)


- Epilepsy
- Autism Spectrum
- Tourette Syndrome
- ADHD
- DYSLEXIA
- Acquired neurodivergencies  
    ex: traumatic brain injury
- FETAL ALCOHOL SPECTRUM
- and many more!



## ADHD
Attention-Deficit Hyperactivity Disorder Tendency to miss details, but may
recognize the main idea or whole picture

#### Common Differences

- Talkative (may blurt out or interrupt)
- High energy: may have a very active body and/or mind
- May get distracted easily. or pay attention to details unrelated to the task at hand.
- Challenges with self-starting (may procrastinate)
- Sensory processing differences: fldgeting or stimming (may become overstimulated or understimulated)
- Time agnosia; challenges with time judgement.
- Organization may be a challenge: commonly are visual organizers
- Emotional sensitivity & emotional regulation challenges (May have Rejection Sensitive Dysphoria, which is Intense sensitivity and emotional pain related to real or perceived rejection or criticism)
