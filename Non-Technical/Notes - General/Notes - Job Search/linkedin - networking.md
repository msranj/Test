
BUILDING YOUR NETWORK ON LINKEDIN, TO FOSTER OPPORTUNITY  
---------------------------------------------------------  
This thread goes into detail on how to build a resilient network with hiring managers and recruiters on Linkedin.  

1. Reach out to hiring managers after viewing their job ads and pitch yourself to them like this:  

"Hey, I saw that job ad you posted. I got three reasons you and I need to have a conversation asap:

	1. Reason 1: - "I want to tell you about this project I built with <job listing languages>"

	2. Reason 2: - "I really admire the work you guys are doing, by doing X/Y/Z."

	3. Reason 3: - "I did X for company Y which resulted in Z and I know you guys are looking for that"

	I look forward to talking with you!" 
    
2. The best way to make a connection is to make it a mutually beneficial relationship where all parties benefit.
    
        The goal is to get a conversation going to open the doors towards building a genuine relationship, where all parties benefit.
    
        Speak like an actual human, and communicate the value you bring in the best way possible. 
        
	    Remember: relationships are important!
