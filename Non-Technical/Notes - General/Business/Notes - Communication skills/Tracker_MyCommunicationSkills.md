## Week 1 — Build Your Foundation
*Focus: unlearn bad habits, diagnose your specific roadblocks*

- [ ] **Day 1 — Baseline Recording**
  Record yourself (phone camera, 2 min) explaining your job to an imaginary new colleague. Don't rewatch yet — just save it as `day1_baseline`. This is your "before" reference for Day 30.
  📝 Journal: What did you notice about your own voice/pace while speaking?

- [ ] **Day 2 — Watch Your Baseline**
  Rewatch Day 1's recording. Note 3 specific things: filler words used, pace (too fast/slow), where you paused or stammered.
  📝 Journal: List your top 3 "roadblocks" in your own words.

- [ ] **Day 3 — Unlearn the Myths**
  Read/reflect on 3 common myths: (1) "I need to sound like someone else to be credible," (2) "Pausing = weakness," (3) "Stammering means I'm not prepared." Write why each is false for you specifically.
  📝 Journal: Which myth has held you back the most?

- [ ] **Day 4 — The Pause Drill**
  Read any paragraph (news article) out loud. Force a full 2-second silent pause at every comma/period. This retrains your brain that silence ≠ failure.
  📝 Journal: How did it feel to pause on purpose?

- [ ] **Day 5 — Diagnostic Wrap-up**
  Re-read your Day 2–4 journal entries. Write a one-paragraph "Communication Diagnosis" — your real roadblocks, not assumed ones.
  📝 Journal: Paste your diagnosis here.

- [ ] **Day 6 — Breath & Voice Warm-up**
  Practice diaphragmatic breathing (4 sec in, 4 sec hold, 6 sec out) x 5, then read a paragraph using breath support instead of shallow chest breathing.
  📝 Journal: Did your voice sound steadier?

- [ ] **Day 7 — Week 1 Review**
  Rate your confidence (1–10). Rewatch Day 1 video once more.
  📝 Journal + Self-Rating: _____ /10

## Week 2 — Speak Fearlessly & Master Body Language
*Focus: this is your core growth area — presentations & stage fear*

- [ ] **Day 8 — Mirror Posture Drill**
  Stand in front of a mirror. Practice "presentation stance" — feet grounded, shoulders back, hands relaxed at sides (not crossed/fidgeting) — for 2 min, then deliver a 1-min impromptu topic (e.g., "why I like my job") while holding the posture.
  📝 Journal: Where did your body tense up?

- [ ] **Day 9 — Eye Contact Drill**
  Practice speaking to 3 fixed points in a room (simulating 3 people in an audience) for a 2-min talk, holding each "gaze point" for a full sentence before shifting.
  📝 Journal: Did fixed eye contact feel more or less scary than expected?

- [ ] **Day 10 — Gesture Practice**
  Record a 2-min talk using deliberate open-hand gestures to emphasize points (no pointing, no pocket-hands). Rewatch with sound off — do gestures look natural?
  📝 Journal: One gesture habit to keep, one to drop.

- [ ] **Day 11 — Name the Fear**
  Write down exactly what you fear happens when you present (e.g., "people will notice I stammer," "I'll go blank"). For each fear, write the realistic worst-case AND how you'd recover.
  📝 Journal: Your top fear + your recovery plan.

- [ ] **Day 12 — Exposure Drill: Solo Stage Simulation**
  Set up a "stage" (even just standing at one end of a room). Deliver a 3-min talk on a topic you know well, standing the whole time, no notes.
  📝 Journal: What % of it felt fear-driven vs. content-driven?

- [ ] **Day 13 — Stammer-Interrupt Technique**
  Practice: the moment you feel a block/stammer coming, pause, breathe, and restart the *phrase* (not the whole sentence) slightly slower. Drill this on 5 sentences you find tricky.
  📝 Journal: Did slowing the restart help?

- [ ] **Day 14 — Week 2 Review + Recording**
  Record a fresh 2-min "explain your job" video (same prompt as Day 1). Compare side-by-side with Day 1.
  📝 Journal + Self-Rating: _____ /10 — what visibly improved?

## Week 3 — The Science of Communication
*Focus: storytelling, persuasion, and message design (Coca-Cola/Cadbury-style case studies)*

- [ ] **Day 15 — Story Spine Basics**
  Learn the basic story spine: Setup → Conflict → Resolution. Rewrite a recent work update you gave using this spine instead of a flat list of facts.
  📝 Journal: Paste your before/after version.

- [ ] **Day 16 — Case Study: Brand Storytelling**
  Look up how one well-known brand (Coca-Cola, Cadbury, or similar) tells emotional stories instead of listing product features. Note 2 techniques they use.
  📝 Journal: One technique you could steal for your next work presentation.

- [ ] **Day 17 — The "So What" Test**
  Take 3 facts from your current work project. For each, ask "so what does this mean for my audience?" and rewrite as an audience-relevant point.
  📝 Journal: Paste your 3 rewritten points.

- [ ] **Day 18 — Message Simplicity Drill**
  Take a complex topic you know and explain it in exactly 3 sentences a non-expert would understand (no jargon).
  📝 Journal: Was cutting jargon harder than expected?

- [ ] **Day 19 — Persuasion Basics**
  Learn the ethos/logos/pathos framework (credibility/logic/emotion). Identify which one you lean on most and which you underuse.
  📝 Journal: Your natural strength + your gap.

- [ ] **Day 20 — Mini-Story Practice**
  Record yourself telling a 90-second personal/work story using Setup → Conflict → Resolution + one emotional beat.
  📝 Journal: Did structure make it easier to speak without stammering?

- [ ] **Day 21 — Week 3 Review**
  Reread Day 15–20 entries.
  📝 Journal + Self-Rating: _____ /10

## Week 4 — Structure, Study Great Speakers & Present Like a Leader

- [ ] **Day 22 — The Opening Hook**
  Study how strong speakers open talks — with a question, a story, or a bold statement, never "today I'll talk about…". Write 3 different hook options for a topic you present often at work.
  📝 Journal: Which hook felt most natural to say out loud?

- [ ] **Day 23 — Crisis → Hope Structure**
  Learn the Crisis–Hope arc (name a problem/tension → offer resolution/hope). Restructure a work topic using this arc.
  📝 Journal: Paste your Crisis→Hope outline.

- [ ] **Day 24 — Analyze a TEDx Talk**
  Watch one TEDx talk (e.g., a Justin Baldoni or Apollo Robbins talk) and note: how they open, how they use pauses, how they close. Write 3 observations.
  📝 Journal: One technique to try next time you present.

- [ ] **Day 25 — Analyze a Thinker-Style Speaker**
  Watch a clip of Naval Ravikant or Dandapani (calm, deliberate speaking style). Note their pacing and use of silence vs. the more animated TEDx style from Day 24.
  📝 Journal: Which style suits your personality more — energetic or calm-deliberate?

- [ ] **Day 26 — Slide Design Basics**
  Learn the "one idea per slide, minimal text" principle used by great presenters. Redesign one slide from a real work deck you have, applying this rule.
  📝 Journal: Before/after — what did you remove?

- [ ] **Day 27 — Executive Presence Traits**
  Research what makes leaders like Jeff Bezos or Tony Fadell effective presenters (clarity, calm authority, story-driven data). Note 2 traits to adopt.
  📝 Journal: Your 2 chosen traits + how you'll practice them.

- [ ] **Day 28 — Full Dry Run**
  Combine everything: pick a real work topic, write a hook + Crisis-Hope structure + 3 clean slides (or bullet points), and rehearse it out loud twice using your posture/eye-contact/pause techniques.
  📝 Journal: What felt most natural now vs. Day 1?

- [ ] **Day 29 — Final Recording**
  Record yourself delivering the Day 28 talk fully, 3–5 min, as if presenting live.
  📝 Journal: Self-critique — 2 wins, 1 thing to keep working on.

- [ ] **Day 30 — Capstone Review**
  Watch Day 1, Day 14, and Day 29 recordings back-to-back. Write a short reflection on your journey.
  📝 Journal + Final Self-Rating: _____ /10
  🎯 **Next steps:** What will you keep practicing after Day 30?
