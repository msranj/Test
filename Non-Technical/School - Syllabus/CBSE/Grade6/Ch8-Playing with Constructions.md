
## Chapter 8: Playing with Constructions

### Concept 8.1 — Using a Ruler and Compass
A **compass** draws circles and arcs of a fixed radius. A **ruler** draws straight lines and measures length.

**Example 1:** Drawing a circle of radius 4 cm using a compass.  
**Example 2:** Drawing a line segment of exactly 7 cm using a ruler.  
**Example 3:** Marking two points 5 cm apart and joining them with a ruler.

### Concept 8.2 — Constructing a Perpendicular Bisector
A line that cuts another line segment into two equal halves at exactly 90°.

<div style="text-align:center">

```
        |
   A————+————B
        |
Perpendicular bisector cuts AB into two equal halves at 90°
```
*Fig 8.1 — Perpendicular bisector of line segment AB*
</div>

**Example 1:** Bisecting a 6 cm line segment gives two 3 cm halves.  
**Example 2:** Constructing the perpendicular bisector of a door's width to find its exact centre.  
**Example 3:** Used to construct the centre point of a garden path.

### Concept 8.3 — Constructing Circles and Angles
**Example 1:** Drawing a circle of radius 3.5 cm with a compass.  
**Example 2:** Constructing a 60° angle using a compass (without a protractor).  
**Example 3:** Constructing two circles of different radii sharing the same centre point.
