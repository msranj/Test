
## Chapter 4: Data Handling and Presentation

### Concept 4.1 — Collecting and Organising Data
**Data** is information (usually numbers) we collect to study something, like the favourite fruit of students in a class.

**Example 1:** Recording how many students like Mango, Apple, or Banana as their favourite fruit.  
**Example 2:** Noting the daily temperature for 7 days of a week.  
**Example 3:** Counting how many cars, bikes, and buses pass a road in one hour.

### Concept 4.2 — Pictographs
A **pictograph** uses pictures/symbols to represent data, where each symbol equals a fixed number of items.

<div style="text-align:center">

```
Mango   🥭🥭🥭        (each 🥭 = 2 students)
Apple   🍎🍎
Banana  🍌🍌🍌🍌
```
*Fig 4.1 — A simple pictograph of favourite fruits*
</div>

**Example 1:** If 🥭 = 2 students and there are 3 mango symbols, that's 6 students who like mango.  
**Example 2:** A pictograph of books read by 4 friends, where 📖 = 1 book each.  
**Example 3:** A pictograph showing rainfall over 4 months, where 💧 = 10mm rain.

### Concept 4.3 — Bar Graphs
A **bar graph** uses bars (rectangles) of different heights/lengths to show and compare data.

**Example 1:** A bar graph comparing marks scored by 5 students in a test.  
**Example 2:** A bar graph showing the number of students in each class section (6A, 6B, 6C).  
**Example 3:** A bar graph comparing the population of 4 different cities.
