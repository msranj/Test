
## Chapter 7: Fractions

*(Already covered in detail in your earlier tutorial — summary below; see the full "Class6_Fractions_Tutorial.pdf" for diagrams and practice questions.)*

### Concept 7.1 — What is a Fraction?
A fraction shows a part of a whole, written as numerator/denominator.

**Example 1:** 1/2 — one part out of 2 equal parts (half a roti)  
**Example 2:** 3/4 — three parts out of 4 equal parts (3 pieces of a 4-slice pizza)  
**Example 3:** 5/8 — five parts out of 8 equal parts

### Concept 7.2 — Equivalent Fractions
Different fractions that represent the same value.

**Example 1:** 1/2 = 2/4  
**Example 2:** 2/3 = 4/6  
**Example 3:** 3/5 = 6/10

### Concept 7.3 — Adding/Subtracting Fractions
Same denominator: add/subtract numerators directly. Different denominators: find a common denominator first.

**Example 1:** 2/7 + 3/7 = 5/7  
**Example 2:** 1/3 + 1/4 = 4/12 + 3/12 = 7/12  
**Example 3:** 3/4 − 1/2 = 3/4 − 2/4 = 1/4
