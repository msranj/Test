

## Chapter 9: Symmetry

### Concept 9.1 — Line of Symmetry
A shape has a **line of symmetry** if it can be folded along a line so both halves match exactly.

<div style="text-align:center">

```
      |
   ▲  |  ▲     (a butterfly shape — symmetric about the middle line)
      |
```
*Fig 9.1 — A shape with one line of symmetry*
</div>

**Example 1:** A square has 4 lines of symmetry.  
**Example 2:** A circle has infinite lines of symmetry (any line through the centre).  
**Example 3:** The letter "A" has 1 line of symmetry (vertical, down the middle).

### Concept 9.2 — Rotational Symmetry
A shape has **rotational symmetry** if it looks the same after being rotated (turned) by some angle less than a full circle.

**Example 1:** A square has rotational symmetry of order 4 (looks the same every 90° turn).  
**Example 2:** An equilateral triangle has rotational symmetry of order 3 (every 120°).  
**Example 3:** A pinwheel/fan blade design has rotational symmetry.
