
## Chapter 6: Perimeter and Area

### Concept 6.1 — Perimeter
**Perimeter** is the total distance around the outside edge (boundary) of a shape.

<div style="text-align:center">

```
   ___5___
  |       |
 3|       |3
  |_______|
      5
Perimeter = 5+5+3+3 = 16 units
```
*Fig 6.1 — Perimeter of a rectangle*
</div>

**Example 1:** A rectangle of length 8 cm and width 3 cm → Perimeter = 2×(8+3) = **22 cm**  
**Example 2:** A square with each side 5 cm → Perimeter = 4×5 = **20 cm**  
**Example 3:** A triangle with sides 4 cm, 5 cm, 6 cm → Perimeter = 4+5+6 = **15 cm**

### Concept 6.2 — Area
**Area** is the amount of flat surface (space) a shape covers, measured in square units.

**Example 1:** A rectangle 6 cm × 4 cm → Area = 6×4 = **24 sq. cm**  
**Example 2:** A square with side 7 cm → Area = 7×7 = **49 sq. cm**  
**Example 3:** A room 5 m long and 4 m wide needs flooring → Area = 5×4 = **20 sq. m** of tiles
