                                                                        
                                                                        
## 14. Prepositions                                                     
                                                                        
                                                                        
### What is a preposition?                                              
                                                                        
                                                                        
A **preposition** is a small word that shows how one thing is connected 
to another. It often tells us where something is, when something happens
or which direction something moves.                                     
                                                                        
                                                                        
### 1. Prepositions of place -- where?                                  
                                                                        
                                                                        
Imagine a toy car on a table: - The car is **on** the table. - The car  
is **under** the table. - The car is **beside** the table. - The car is 
**behind** the table. - The car is **between** two books. - The car is  
**in** the box.                                                         
                                                                        
                                                                        
### 2. Prepositions of time -- when?                                    
                                                                        
                                                                        
-   **at** 7 o'clock                                                    
-   **on** Monday                                                       
-   **in** June                                                         
-   **in** the morning                                                  
-   **before** lunch                                                    
-   **after** school                                                    
                                                                        
                                                                        
A helpful guide: - **at** -- an exact time: at 6:30 - **on** -- a day or
date: on Friday, on 12 June - **in** -- a month, year or part of the    
day: in July, in 2026, in the evening                                   
                                                                        
                                                                        
### 3. Prepositions of direction or movement                            
                                                                        
                                                                        
-   Walk **to** the gate.                                               
-   The bird flew **into** the room.                                    
-   The bus went **through** the tunnel.                                
-   We walked **across** the road.                                      
-   The ball rolled **towards** the door.                               
                                                                        
                                                                        
### Everyday example                                                    
                                                                        
                                                                        
When you say, "I put my lunch box **inside** my school bag **before**   
leaving **for** school," the prepositions explain where and when things 
happen.                                                                 
                                                                        
                                                                        
### Try it                                                              
                                                                        
                                                                        
Choose a suitable preposition. 1. The picture is \_\_\_\_\_ the wall.   
(on/in) 2. We go to school \_\_\_\_\_ Monday. (on/at) 3. The keys are   
\_\_\_\_\_ the drawer. (in/at) 4. The train passed \_\_\_\_\_ the       
tunnel. (through/under) 5. The class begins \_\_\_\_\_ 9 o'clock.       
(at/in)                                                                 
                                                                        
                                                                        
**Answers:** 1. on 2. on 3. in 4. through 5. at
