


## 15. Adverbs I: Manner, Place and Time


### What is an adverb?


An **adverb** gives us more information about an action. It can tell us
how, where or when something happens.


Think about a student walking into class: - She walked **quietly**.
(How?) - She waited **outside**. (Where?) - She arrived **early**.
(When?)


### 1. Adverbs of manner -- how?


They tell us how an action is done. - The baby smiled **happily**. 
- The tortoise walked **slowly**. - Please write **neatly**. 
- The boy
answered **politely**.


Many adverbs of manner end in **-ly**, but not all do: 
- The car moved **fast**. 
- The child sang **well**.


### 2. Adverbs of place -- where?


They tell us where an action happens. 
- Come **here**. 
- The children are playing **outside**. 
- The birds flew **away**. 
- Please look **upstairs**.


### 3. Adverbs of time -- when?


They tell us when an action happens. 
- We will leave **tomorrow**. 
- I finished my work **yesterday**. 
- The bus arrived **early**. 
- Dad is coming **soon**.


### A quick way to find them


Ask a question about the action: 
- How did she speak? **Softly.** 
- Where did they play? **Outside.** 
- When did he arrive? **Yesterday.**


### Try it


Name the type of adverb in bold. 
1. The girl danced **gracefully**. 
2. Please come **inside**. 
3. We will visit Grandma **tomorrow**. 
4. The horse ran **fast**. 
5. The children are waiting **here**.


**Answers:** 1. Manner 2. Place 3. Time 4. Manner 5. Place
