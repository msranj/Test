## 1. Compound Nouns

### What is a compound noun?

A **compound noun** is a naming word made by joining two or more words.
The new word names one person, place, animal, thing or idea.

Think of it like making a new toy by joining two building blocks. The
two words come together to make one name.

### Everyday examples

-   **tooth + brush = toothbrush** -- something we use to clean our
    teeth.
-   **rain + coat = raincoat** -- something we wear in the rain.
-   **class + room = classroom** -- the room where we learn.
-   **sun + flower = sunflower** -- a flower that looks towards the sun.

### Three ways compound nouns can be written

1.  **One word:** football, bedroom, toothpaste
2.  **Two separate words:** bus stop, dining table, swimming pool
3.  **Words joined with a hyphen (-):** mother-in-law, check-in

### Remember

The words work together to name one thing. For example, a *toothbrush*
is one object, not a tooth and a brush being discussed separately.
