
                                                                        
                                                                        
## 13. Adjectives II: Degrees of Comparison                             
                                                                        
                                                                        
### Why do we compare?                                                  
                                                                        
                                                                        
We compare things when we want to say that one is tall, taller or the   
tallest. Adjectives can show three degrees of comparison.               
                                                                        
                                                                        
Imagine three towers made from building blocks: - Tower A is **tall**. -
Tower B is **taller** than Tower A. - Tower C is the **tallest** of all 
three.                                                                  
                                                                        
                                                                        
### The three degrees                                                   
                                                                        
                                                                        
1.  **Positive:** describes one person or thing -- tall.                
2.  **Comparative:** compares two -- taller.                            
3.  **Superlative:** compares three or more -- tallest.                 
                                                                        
                                                                        
### How to form them                                                    
                                                                        
                                                                        
  Positive    Comparative      Superlative                              
  ----------- ---------------- ----------------                         
  small       smaller          smallest                                 
  tall        taller           tallest                                  
  nice        nicer            nicest                                   
  happy       happier          happiest                                 
  big         bigger           biggest                                  
  beautiful   more beautiful   most beautiful                           
  good        better           best                                     
  bad         worse            worst                                    
                                                                        
                                                                        
### Useful rules                                                        
                                                                        
                                                                        
-   Many short adjectives take **-er** and **-est**: fast, faster,      
    fastest.                                                            
-   If an adjective ends in **y**, it often changes to **i**: happy,    
    happier, happiest.                                                  
-   Some short words double the last letter: big, bigger, biggest.      
-   Longer adjectives often use **more** and **most**: careful, more    
    careful, most careful.                                              
-   Some words change completely: good, better, best.                   
                                                                        
                                                                        
### Use them correctly                                                  
                                                                        
                                                                        
-   "My bag is **heavier than** yours." (comparing two)                 
-   "This is the **heaviest** bag in the class." (comparing more than   
    two)                                                                
-   "A mango is **sweet**." (describing one)                            
                                                                        
                                                                        
Avoid using two comparison forms together: - Correct: more beautiful -  
Incorrect: more beautifuler                                             
                                                                        
                                                                        
### Try it                                                              
                                                                        
                                                                        
1.  Write the comparative form of **small**.                            
2.  Write the superlative form of **happy**.                            
3.  Choose: This road is (longer/longest) than that road.               
4.  Choose: Mount Everest is the (higher/highest) mountain in the world.
5.  Complete: good, better, \_\_\_\_\_.                                 
                                                                        
                                                                        
**Answers:** 1. smaller 2. happiest 3. longer 4. highest 5. best
