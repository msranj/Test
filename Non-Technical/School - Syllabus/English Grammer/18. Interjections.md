
                                                                        
                                                                        
## 18. Interjections                                                    
                                                                        
                                                                        
### What is an interjection?                                            
                                                                        
                                                                        
An **interjection** is a short word or sound that shows a sudden feeling
or reaction.                                                            
                                                                        
                                                                        
Imagine dropping your ice cream: - "**Oh no!** My ice cream fell!"      
Imagine winning a race: - "**Hurray!** We won!"                         
                                                                        
                                                                        
### Common interjections                                                
                                                                        
                                                                        
  Interjection   Feeling or use       Example                           
  -------------- -------------------- -------------------------------   
  Hurray!        joy                  Hurray! Our team won!             
  Ouch!          pain                 Ouch! I hurt my toe.              
  Wow!           surprise or wonder   Wow! What a huge rainbow!         
  Alas!          sadness              Alas! The plant has dried up.     
  Phew!          relief               Phew! We caught the bus.          
  Oops!          a small mistake      Oops! I dropped my pencil.        
  Hey!           calling attention    Hey! Wait for me!                 
  Hush!          asking for silence   Hush! The baby is sleeping.       
                                                                        
                                                                        
### Punctuation                                                         
                                                                        
                                                                        
An interjection often has an exclamation mark (!) after it when the     
feeling is strong. - "Wow! That is amazing!" Sometimes a comma is used  
when the feeling is mild: - "Well, let us begin."                       
                                                                        
                                                                        
### Remember                                                            
                                                                        
                                                                        
An interjection shows a feeling. It does not usually join the main parts
of a sentence.                                                          
                                                                        
                                                                        
### Try it                                                              
                                                                        
                                                                        
Choose a suitable interjection. 1. You hurt your knee. "\_\_\_\_\_! That
hurts." 2. Your team wins. "\_\_\_\_\_! We won." 3. You finish a        
difficult task. "\_\_\_\_\_! It is finally done." 4. You see a beautiful
rainbow. "\_\_\_\_\_! Look at that." 5. You make a small mistake.       
"\_\_\_\_\_! I forgot my book."                                         
                                                                        
                                                                        
**Answers:** 1. Ouch 2. Hurray 3. Phew 4. Wow 5. Oops
