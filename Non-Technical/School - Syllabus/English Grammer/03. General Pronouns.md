
                                                                        
                                                                        
## 3. General Pronouns                                                  
                                                                        
                                                                        
### What is a pronoun?                                                  
                                                                        
                                                                        
A **pronoun** is a word we use instead of a naming word (noun). It helps
us avoid repeating the same name again and again.                       
                                                                        
                                                                        
Example: - Without a pronoun: "Asha has a bag. Asha keeps Asha's books  
in the bag." - With pronouns: "Asha has a bag. **She** keeps **her**    
books in it."                                                           
                                                                        
                                                                        
### Useful kinds of pronouns                                            
                                                                        
                                                                        
**1. Personal pronouns** -- name the person or people speaking,         
listening or being spoken about. - I, me, we, us, you, he, him, she,    
her, it, they, them                                                     
                                                                        
                                                                        
**2. Possessive pronouns** -- show that something belongs to someone. - 
mine, ours, yours, his, hers, theirs                                    
                                                                        
                                                                        
**3. Demonstrative pronouns** -- point to people or things. - this,     
that, these, those - "**This** is my pencil." "**Those** are your       
shoes."                                                                 
                                                                        
                                                                        
**4. Interrogative pronouns** -- help us ask questions. - who, whom,    
whose, which, what - "**Who** is at the door?" "**Which** is your       
bottle?"                                                                
                                                                        
                                                                        
**5. Reflexive pronouns** -- refer back to the person doing the         
action. - myself, yourself, himself, herself, itself, ourselves,        
yourselves, themselves - "I packed my school bag **myself**."           
                                                                        
                                                                        
**6. Indefinite pronouns** -- refer to people or things without saying  
exactly who or what. - someone, anyone, everyone, nobody, something,    
anything, everything - "**Someone** left a lunch box here."             
                                                                        
                                                                        
### Remember                                                            
                                                                        
                                                                        
A pronoun takes the place of a noun. Check that your reader can         
understand who or what the pronoun refers to.                           
                                                                        
                                                                        
### Try it                                                              
                                                                        
                                                                        
1.  Replace "Rohan" with a pronoun: "Rohan is reading."                 
2.  Which word is a pronoun? "The cat is sleeping. It is tired."        
3.  Choose a pronoun: "\_\_\_\_\_ is knocking at the door." (Who /      
    Table)                                                              
4.  Find the pronoun: "These are my crayons."                           
5.  Complete: "I made this card by \_\_\_\_\_."                         
                                                                        
                                                                        
**Answers:** 1. He 2. It 3. Who 4. These 5. myself
