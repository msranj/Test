
                                                                        
                                                                        
## 19. Direct and Indirect Speech                                       
                                                                        
                                                                        
### What is speech?                                                     
                                                                        
                                                                        
Speech is the way people say something. We can report their words in two
main ways.                                                              
                                                                        
                                                                        
### Direct speech                                                       
                                                                        
                                                                        
Direct speech gives the speaker's exact words. We put the exact words   
inside quotation marks (" ").                                           
                                                                        
                                                                        
Example: - Riya said, "I am hungry." - Dad said, "The bus is here."     
                                                                        
                                                                        
The quotation marks show the words spoken by the person.                
                                                                        
                                                                        
### Indirect speech                                                     
                                                                        
                                                                        
Indirect speech tells us what someone said, but does not repeat the     
exact words.                                                            
                                                                        
                                                                        
Example: - Direct: Riya said, "I am hungry." - Indirect: Riya said that 
she was hungry.                                                         
                                                                        
                                                                        
We do not use quotation marks in indirect speech.                       
                                                                        
                                                                        
### What may change?                                                    
                                                                        
                                                                        
When we report someone's words, we may need to change: 1. **The         
pronoun:** "I" may change to "he" or "she", depending on who spoke. 2.  
**The verb:** "am" may change to "was" when reporting in the past. 3.   
**Time words:** "today" may change to "that day" when needed.           
                                                                        
                                                                        
Examples: - Direct: Aman said, "I am tired." - Indirect: Aman said that 
he was tired. - Direct: Mother said, "I will cook dinner." - Indirect:  
Mother said that she would cook dinner. - Direct: Neha said, "I like    
mangoes." - Indirect: Neha said that she liked mangoes.                 
                                                                        
                                                                        
### Reporting questions and requests                                    
                                                                        
                                                                        
-   Direct: The teacher asked, "Are you ready?"                         
-   Indirect: The teacher asked if we were ready.                       
-   Direct: Father said, "Please close the door."                       
-   Indirect: Father asked me to close the door.                        
                                                                        
                                                                        
### A helpful reminder                                                  
                                                                        
                                                                        
Always ask: **Who said it?** This helps you choose the correct pronoun. 
                                                                        
                                                                        
### Try it                                                              
                                                                        
                                                                        
Change these into indirect speech. 1. Rohan said, "I am happy." 2. Meena
said, "I like music." 3. Dad said, "I will help you." 4. The teacher    
asked, "Are you ready?" 5. Mother said, "Please wash your hands."       
                                                                        
                                                                        
**Sample answers:** 1. Rohan said that he was happy. 2. Meena said that 
she liked music. 3. Dad said that he would help me/us. *(The correct    
pronoun depends on whom Dad was speaking to.)* 4. The teacher asked if  
we were ready. 5. Mother asked me to wash my hands.
