
                                                                        
                                                                        
## 12. Adjectives I: Qualitative, Quantitative and Demonstrative        
                                                                        
                                                                        
### What is an adjective?                                               
                                                                        
                                                                        
An **adjective** is a describing word. It gives us more information     
about a naming word.                                                    
                                                                        
                                                                        
Imagine describing your school bag: - a **blue** bag - a **heavy** bag -
**two** bags - **this** bag                                             
                                                                        
                                                                        
The words in bold help us understand which bag or what kind of bag.     
                                                                        
                                                                        
### 1. Qualitative adjectives -- what kind?                             
                                                                        
                                                                        
These adjectives tell us about a quality or feature. - a **kind**       
teacher - a **sweet** mango - a **round** ball - a **bright** classroom 
                                                                        
                                                                        
Ask: **What kind?**                                                     
                                                                        
                                                                        
### 2. Quantitative adjectives -- how much or how many?                 
                                                                        
                                                                        
These tell us about an amount or number. - **five** pencils - **many**  
students - **some** water - **little** sugar                            
                                                                        
                                                                        
Ask: **How much?** or **How many?**                                     
                                                                        
                                                                        
### 3. Demonstrative adjectives -- which one?                           
                                                                        
                                                                        
These point out a particular naming word. - **this** book (near, one) - 
**that** tree (far, one) - **these** pencils (near, more than one) -    
**those** birds (far, more than one)                                    
                                                                        
                                                                        
Ask: **Which one or which ones?**                                       
                                                                        
                                                                        
### Remember the four pointing words                                    
                                                                        
                                                                        
  Word    Use it for                         Example                    
  ------- ---------------------------------- ---------------            
  this    one thing near                     this pencil                
  that    one thing farther away             that building              
  these   more than one thing near           these flowers              
  those   more than one thing farther away   those stars                
                                                                        
                                                                        
### Try it                                                              
                                                                        
                                                                        
Name the type of adjective in bold. 1. We saw a **beautiful** butterfly.
2. There are **seven** days in a week. 3. **Those** shoes are new. 4.   
Please give me **some** rice. 5. It was a **cold** morning.             
                                                                        
                                                                        
**Answers:** 1. Qualitative 2. Quantitative 3. Demonstrative 4.         
Quantitative 5. Qualitative
