                                                                        
                                                                        
## 2. Countable and Uncountable Nouns                                   
                                                                        
                                                                        
### Countable nouns                                                     
                                                                        
                                                                        
A **countable noun** names something we can count one by one. It has a  
singular form and a plural form.                                        
                                                                        
                                                                        
Examples: one pencil, two pencils; one mango, five mangoes; one chair,  
four chairs.                                                            
                                                                        
                                                                        
We can use **a/an** with one countable item: - a book - an orange       
                                                                        
                                                                        
We can use numbers with countable nouns: - three notebooks - ten        
students                                                                
                                                                        
                                                                        
### Uncountable nouns                                                   
                                                                        
                                                                        
An **uncountable noun** names something we usually measure, rather than 
count one by one.                                                       
                                                                        
                                                                        
Examples: water, milk, rice, sugar, sand, air.                          
                                                                        
                                                                        
We do not usually say *two waters* or *three rices* when we mean the    
substance. We can say: - a glass of water - two bottles of milk - a bowl
of rice - three spoonfuls of sugar                                      
                                                                        
                                                                        
### A simple kitchen example                                            
                                                                        
                                                                        
Imagine making breakfast. You can count **eggs** (one egg, two eggs),   
but you usually measure **milk** in a glass or cup.                     
                                                                        
                                                                        
### Words to use                                                        
                                                                        
                                                                        
-   Countable: **many**, a few, several                                 
-   Uncountable: **much**, a little, some                               
-   Both: **some** and **a lot of** can be used with both, depending on 
    the sentence.                                                       
                                                                        
                                                                        
Examples: - How **many** apples are there? - How **much** water is in   
the bottle? - We have **some** bananas. - There is **some** rice.       
                                                                        
                                                                        
### Remember                                                            
                                                                        
                                                                        
Some nouns can be used in different ways depending on what we mean. For 
example, *chicken* can mean the animal (countable: three chickens) or   
the meat (uncountable: some chicken).                                   
                                                                        
                                                                        
### Try it                                                              
                                                                        
                                                                        
Write **C** for countable and **U** for uncountable. 1. notebook 2.     
honey 3. chairs 4. flour 5. grapes                                      
                                                                        
                                                                        
Choose the correct word: 6. How (many/much) juice is left? 7. How       
(many/much) students are in your class?                                 
                                                                        
                                                                        
**Answers:** 1. C 2. U 3. C 4. U 5. C 6. much 7. many
