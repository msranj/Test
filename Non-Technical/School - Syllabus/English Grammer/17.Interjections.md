
                                                                        
                                                                        
## 17. Conjunctions                                                     
                                                                        
                                                                        
### What is a conjunction?                                              
                                                                        
                                                                        
A **conjunction** is a joining word. It joins words or groups of words  
and helps our sentences flow.                                           
                                                                        
                                                                        
Imagine two train coaches. A conjunction is like the connector that     
joins them.                                                             
                                                                        
                                                                        
### Common conjunctions                                                 
                                                                        
                                                                        
**And** -- adds information. - I packed a pencil **and** an eraser.     
                                                                        
                                                                        
**But** -- shows a difference. - It was raining, **but** we played      
indoors.                                                                
                                                                        
                                                                        
**Or** -- gives a choice. - Would you like milk **or** juice?           
                                                                        
                                                                        
**Because** -- gives a reason. - I carried an umbrella **because** it   
was raining.                                                            
                                                                        
                                                                        
**So** -- shows a result. - I was hungry, **so** I ate a sandwich.      
                                                                        
                                                                        
**If** -- shows a condition. - **If** you finish your work, you can     
play.                                                                   
                                                                        
                                                                        
**Although** -- shows an unexpected difference. - **Although** it was   
cold, we went for a walk.                                               
                                                                        
                                                                        
**When, before, after** -- connect actions with time. - Wash your hands 
**before** you eat. - We cheered **when** our team won. - We went home  
**after** the match.                                                    
                                                                        
                                                                        
### Choose the meaning                                                  
                                                                        
                                                                        
-   and = adds                                                          
-   but = contrasts                                                     
-   or = offers a choice                                                
-   because = gives a reason                                            
-   so = gives a result                                                 
                                                                        
                                                                        
### Try it                                                              
                                                                        
                                                                        
Choose the best conjunction. 1. I like apples \_\_\_\_\_ bananas.       
(and/but) 2. Wear your coat \_\_\_\_\_ it is cold. (because/or) 3. Would
you like tea \_\_\_\_\_ milk? (or/so) 4. It was late, \_\_\_\_\_ we went
home. (so/but) 5. I wanted to play, \_\_\_\_\_ I had homework. (but/and)
                                                                        
                                                                        
**Answers:** 1. and 2. because 3. or 4. so 5. but
