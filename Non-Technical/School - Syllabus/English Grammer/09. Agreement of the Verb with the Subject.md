                                                                        
                                                                        
## 9. Agreement of the Verb with the Subject                            
                                                                        
                                                                        
### What does it mean?                                                  
                                                                        
                                                                        
A sentence has a **subject** (who or what we are talking about) and a   
**verb** (the action or state). The verb must match the subject.        
                                                                        
                                                                        
Think of it like matching a pair of shoes: the two parts must go        
together.                                                               
                                                                        
                                                                        
### The basic rule                                                      
                                                                        
                                                                        
-   With **he, she, it** or one person/thing, we usually add **-s** or  
    **-es** to the verb in the simple present.                          
-   With **I, you, we, they** or more than one person/thing, we use the 
    base form of the verb.                                              
                                                                        
                                                                        
  Subject    Correct sentence                                           
  ---------- --------------------                                       
  I          I play football.                                           
  You        You play football.                                         
  He         He plays football.                                         
  She        She watches TV.                                            
  The dog    The dog barks.                                             
  We         We play football.                                          
  The dogs   The dogs bark.                                             
                                                                        
                                                                        
### Special examples                                                    
                                                                        
                                                                        
-   I **am** happy.                                                     
-   He **is** happy.                                                    
-   They **are** happy.                                                 
-   She **has** a kite.                                                 
-   We **have** kites.                                                  
-   The child **does** the work.                                        
-   The children **do** the work.                                       
                                                                        
                                                                        
### Watch out                                                           
                                                                        
                                                                        
A subject can have extra words after it. Find the main person or        
thing: - "The **box** of crayons **is** on the table." The subject is   
*box*, not *crayons*. - "My **friends** are coming." The subject is     
*friends*.                                                              
                                                                        
                                                                        
### Try it                                                              
                                                                        
                                                                        
Choose the correct verb. 1. The boy (run/runs) fast. 2. My friends      
(is/are) helpful. 3. The cat (sleep/sleeps) on the sofa. 4. I (has/have)
a new notebook. 5. The basket of apples (is/are) heavy.                 
                                                                        
                                                                        
**Answers:** 1. runs 2. are 3. sleeps 4. have 5. is
