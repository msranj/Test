                                                                        
                                                                        
## 8. Have                                                              
                                                                        
                                                                        
### What does "have" mean?                                              
                                                                        
                                                                        
We use **have** and **has** to talk about owning or having something,   
and to talk about experiences or activities.                            
                                                                        
                                                                        
Examples: - I **have** a new school bag. - She **has** a blue water     
bottle. - We **have** lunch at school.                                  
                                                                        
                                                                        
### Have or has?                                                        
                                                                        
                                                                        
  Use **have** with   Use **has** with                                  
  ------------------- -------------------------------------             
  I                   he                                                
  you                 she                                               
  we                  it                                                
  they                one person or thing: Rohan, the dog               
                                                                        
                                                                        
Examples: - I **have** two pencils. - You **have** a lovely smile. - The
children **have** new books. - My sister **has** a doll. - The school   
**has** a playground.                                                   
                                                                        
                                                                        
### Using "had"                                                         
                                                                        
                                                                        
**Had** is used with all subjects to talk about having something in the 
past. - I **had** a red bicycle. - She **had** a fever yesterday. - They
**had** a picnic last Sunday.                                           
                                                                        
                                                                        
### Questions and negative sentences                                    
                                                                        
                                                                        
-   **Do** you have a pencil?                                           
-   **Does** he have a pencil?                                          
-   I **do not (don't)** have a pencil.                                 
-   She **does not (doesn't)** have a pencil.                           
-   **Did** they have a holiday yesterday?                              
                                                                        
                                                                        
Notice: after **does** or **did**, use **have**, not *has* or *had*. -  
Correct: Does she **have** a pet? - Correct: Did he **have** breakfast? 
                                                                        
                                                                        
### Have/has as helping words                                           
                                                                        
                                                                        
We also use have/has with a past participle: - I **have finished**. - He
**has gone** home.                                                      
                                                                        
                                                                        
### Try it                                                              
                                                                        
                                                                        
1.  My brother (have/has) a cricket bat.                                
2.  We (have/has) English today.                                        
3.  She (have/had) a cold yesterday.                                    
4.  Does Riya (has/have) a sister?                                      
5.  They have \_\_\_\_\_ (eat) their lunch.                             
                                                                        
                                                                        
**Answers:** 1. has 2. have 3. had 4. have 5. eaten
