# Personality development and self-improvement books

These books should change how you think and behave, not just what you know.
Example: 
- Communication skills
- Leadership
- Confidence
- Public speaking
- Time management
- Negotiation
- Decision-making
- Personal finance habits
- Relationships and interpersonal skills


## Prompt 1: Personal transformation

Act as an experienced personal development coach and behavioural learning specialist.

Analyse this book and extract all the lessons that can help me improve my personality, thinking, behaviour, and everyday decisions.

For every major lesson:

Explain the underlying principle.
Explain why it matters.
Give a relatable real-life example.
Explain how it affects personal and professional life.
Identify behaviours I should develop.
Identify behaviours I should avoid.
Suggest a practical exercise.
Explain how to measure improvement.

Create a personal transformation roadmap based on the book.

Divide it into:

Self-awareness
Mindset
Communication
Emotional management
Relationships
Professional behaviour
Daily habits

Do not give generic motivational advice. Base the learning on the actual content of the book.

## Prompt 2: Develop communication and leadership skills
Act as an executive communication and leadership coach.

Extract every important communication, interpersonal, influence, and leadership lesson from this book.

Teach me how to apply these lessons in situations such as:

Talking to strangers.
Communicating with colleagues.
Handling difficult conversations.
Expressing disagreement respectfully.
Presenting ideas confidently.
Handling criticism.
Negotiating.
Leading a team.
Managing conflicts.
Building professional relationships.

For each situation, provide:

The relevant lesson from the book.
An incorrect approach.
A better approach.
A realistic conversation example.
A practical exercise.

Create role-play scenarios so I can practise.



## Prompt 3: Turn lessons into daily habits
Act as a behavioural change coach.

Extract all practical habits and behavioural principles from this book.

Convert them into a realistic daily routine.

Create:

Morning habits.
Workplace habits.
Social interaction habits.
Evening reflection habits.
Weekly improvement exercises.

For every habit, explain:

Why it matters.
How to practise it.
How long to practise it.
Common obstacles.
How to track progress.

Build a 30-day habit development programme.

Make the programme realistic and sustainable. Do not recommend adopting too many habits simultaneously.
