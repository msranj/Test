If I were in my 30s or 40s right now and wanted to leverage AI to retire within 10 years, here's what I'd do:

### 1. Immediately form an LLC company. Not next month. Not once you're 'ready.' This week.

### 2. Select markets with existing cash flow.

For example: 

    online coaches, 
    marketing agencies, 
    SaaS companies, 
    course creators, 
    consultants, 
    B2B service agencies, 
    tax advisors, or 
    recruitment firms.


### 3. Download the Growman browser extension.

Create a list of 25 people who are already selling services in your chosen market.

Add their IG accounts to Growman and scrape all of their followers.

### 4. Use AutoLead AI to contact these businesses.

Use this AI tool to send 100-200 automated DMs daily to book meetings.

If you're just starting out, sending 100 targeted DMs per day can secure at least 1 meeting.

### 5. Create your offer before the first meeting.

Don't complicate things. Every business owner wants to make more money.

Your offer should be like this: 'I book 10-20 additional sales calls each month for online coaches by posting content on LI, IG, or X.'

### 6. Understand mathematical logic.

100 DMs = 1 sales call. Even if you're not good at talking, you can get at least one $5,000/month client from 5-7 sales calls.

Don't charge less than $5,000/month. To reach $10,000/month, you need 2 clients, not 5.

### 7. How to Deliver Results.

Find 20-30 social media accounts in your chosen niche.

Extract their top-performing posts, analyze them using ChatGPT or other AI tools, and then recreate these proven formats for your clients in their own voice.

### 8. Repeat the process.

After acquiring your first customer, continue sending DMs. After that, everything will become easier.

You'll start getting referrals from existing customers and can begin building a small team to handle client deliveries.

### The opportunity in AI is real.

Millions of businesses don't know how to turn social media into customers.

Or don't have the time to do it themselves.

That's your opportunity.
