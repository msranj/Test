#### Step 1: How to Set Up Your Meta Business Account
-----------------------
a) Create a Meta Business Portfolio which encompasses
1. Facebook Pages
2. Instagram accounts
3. ad accounts

- go to business.facebook.com and create account.
- provide all the business name & its details.
- from the settings->page-> Add page with details
- connect instagram accounts.
- add ad account
- add payment method.

b) how Meta Ads Manager structures your ads?
there are 3 levels to this.

1. Campaign (what you want your audience to do,i.e. visiting site,reading post,purchase )
2. Ad set (define audience, placement)
3. actual ad (what audience see at their end)


#### step2: Choosing campaign objective

- picking your ad objective.
- buy with your ad, you need to measure (metrics) and improve on with right way.

 Below are the objective
 1. Awareness - spreading the word.
 2. Traffic - Direct viewers
 3. Engaement - get interactions (like, comments)
 4. Get Leads - get customer info (forms inside ads)
 5. Sales - Conversion & Purchases
 6. App Promotion - get app installs

 for beginners, start with
    
    - Traffic: Directs viewers
    - Engagement: get interactions

#### Step7: Optimize your campaign
- once your ad is live, you should monitor & optimize.
    1. Reach: how many unique people saw Ad
    2. Impression: no of times ads were on screen.
    3. CTR: % of impressions where a click(s) occured out of total no of impressions. if less < 1%, rework on audience target.
