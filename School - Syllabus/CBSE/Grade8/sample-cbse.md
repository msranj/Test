## Structural Notes
 
- **Grade 5** has no separate 'Social Science' subject — geography/civic/cultural content is integrated into EVS (*Our Wondrous World*). Science is likewise EVS-integrated, not a standalone subject.
- **Grades 6-8**: Science is one integrated subject (not split into separate Physics/Chemistry/Biology subjects), but each chapter is tagged by **Strand** (Physics / Chemistry / Biology / Earth Science / General Science) for easy filtering.
- **Grade 9**: Science remains a single textbook (*Exploration*) but strand-tagging becomes more exam-relevant as students approach Grade 10 boards.
